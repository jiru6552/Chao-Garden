- Original garden visual meshes were reconstructed from the supplied PRS/REL files and inspected from overhead and oblique views. This supports the limited Neutral/Hero cliff-foot terraces; it is not an original executable session or a complete terrain port.

Review limits: representative desktop views at the existing native renderer settings; no Android, alternative shader, every FOV, every evolved outfit, or exhaustive animation matrix claim. Existing native action probes also completed eating, planting and watering during the final client run.
