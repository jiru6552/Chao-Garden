# Ordinary evolution and honest comparison limits - 0.20

The earlier -1/+1 demo already reached the ordinary renderer's true endpoint caps. `ChaoMeshAsset.development` clamps ordinary Swim/Fly and Run/Power weights to [-1, 1], so assigning +/-3 alone produced the same geometry. The weak visual contrast came from subtle authored morphs, large primary crests that hid generic added ridges, and small multi-model views.

`art/refine_evolution_020.py` refines all fifteen ordinary primary/alignment rigs. It replaces generic ridges with paired swept Swim fins, rising Fly horns, five swept Run quills, and a blunt stepped Power crown. All added slopes are made from axis-aligned rectangular blocks. It also morphs the original primary crest in vertex space, retaining source bone pivots, hierarchy and animations. The core head expands upward/backward while its lower attachment and eye plane remain fixed; eye panels and irises remain aligned. Dormant parts remain nondegenerate but hidden inside the head.

Nine endpoint meshes per rig remain bilinearly blended across the ordinary axes. No ordinary endpoint is relabeled as extended gameplay. Monster evolution is a separate opt-in server rule: over-one influence requires at least 2,000 relevant points, while stage two requires at least 3,000. The showcase uses magnitude three and 3,200 points for Swim/Fly/Run/Power, or balanced Normal with 4,000 Stamina points to reach influence three through its declared Minecraft projection. Plain coats expose geometry in ordinary comparisons.

The independent validator checks 135 distinct signatures, connected nondegenerate block topology, source semantic joints, face planes, and at least ten-percent projected head silhouette change in front or profile for every ordinary axis maximum. It excludes hidden emotion glyphs and the disabled Monster crown from head silhouettes. Closed-cap metadata comes from each actual donor skeleton, retaining authored additions. In particular, aliased Swim/Run/Fly/Power rigs no longer inherit unrelated Baby cube indices that omitted crests or named the skull core/eyes. The independent guard checks every donor crest subtree and protects the core and iris panels.

The separate Normal Monster crown is rebased to its lower attachment edge, so its stage scaling grows upward without lifting its base off the head. Both amplification stages are checked for a continuous attachment. Repeating the finalizer must leave resources byte-identical. Actual Minecraft front/profile captures remain required because silhouette checks do not establish texture quality, animated fit or final visual appeal.

Twenty raw comparison rigs and eight supplied source clips are separate and unchanged. Source provenance remains in ASSET_CREDITS_015.md; the new shapes are authored Minecraft gameplay adaptations.
