# Chao Island gallery study - 0.18

Review date: 30 October 2026. Priority is Sonic Adventure 2 / Sonic Adventure 2 Battle; Dreamcast or other editions are identified where their examples differ.

## Coverage

All **35** distinct pages in the gallery's left navigation were fetched and read. Every one of its **29 image-bearing pages** had representative images visually inspected: **388 gallery-entry samples**, representing **354 unique image URLs**. The counts are recorded in [gallery-coverage.json](../validation-0.18/gallery-coverage.json), including the exact sampled zero-based indices. This is a review of every tab and representative permutations, not a claim to have visually examined all 2,176 distinct image URLs indexed by those pages.

The landing page says the gallery contains 2,225 pictures. Its retrieved pages expose 4,094 image entries and 2,176 distinct image URLs because illustrations are reused across categories; these are an HTML inventory, not a claim to archive the whole service. The selector itself says it is not yet reimplemented. Four face pages are under construction and contain placeholders instead of actual image examples. Those gaps are reported rather than filled with invented screenshots.

The web-search service was unavailable during this review. Public pages and image files were retrieved directly from their Chao Island URLs after an initial transient 403; subsequent ordinary requests with a browser User-Agent succeeded. Working reference copies and contact sheets remain outside the mod source and are not redistributed with the mod.

## Visual findings used in this release

The main Normal, Swim, Fly, Run and Power tabs show that the head silhouette, hands, feet, wings and orb treatment depend on alignment and development. The pictured neutral Regular two-tone child has a blue body, yellow tip and extremities, and pink back wings. Swim has a low broad head and paddles, Fly has swept head lobes and larger back wings, Run has rearward quills, and Power has broader outward head points and a heavier upper body. These are game references, not a claim that every shape is present in the supplied Marketplace pack.

Mono-tone and jewel examples keep their limbs within the same coat family; lighting and painted highlights can make extremities lighter. They should not accidentally inherit unrelated default blue/yellow UV regions. Two-tone is different: it intentionally retains alignment/form accents, and a Dark two-tone can invert or strongly alter the apparent base colour. A uniform tint over every pixel is therefore insufficient for both modes. Eye, mouth, wing-alpha and emotion patches must retain their intended masks.

Targeted follow-up comparisons also checked the regular mono-tone and Red mono/two-tone Chaos examples. Regular mono-tone Chaos is white across all three families. Coloured Light/Angel Chaos retains its coat hue throughout the skin, while two-tone Devil retains a very dark body background and coloured extremities; mono-tone Devil takes the whole coat colour. The custom native eye patches use green for Light, cyan for Angel and yellow for Devil, retaining original alpha. Their source examples are [Light](https://chao-island.com/assets/images/gallery/twotone/chaos/neutralchaostwotoneregular.jpg), [Angel](https://chao-island.com/assets/images/gallery/twotone/chaos/herochaostwotoneregular.jpg) and [Devil](https://chao-island.com/assets/images/gallery/twotone/chaos/darkchaostwotoneregular.jpg).

The three Chaos forms retain their anatomy, eye colours and aura while accepting inherited coat variants. Direct examples reviewed include Blue mono-tone Light Chaos, Pink mono-tone Angel Chaos, Aquamarine jewel Angel Chaos and Ruby jewel Devil Chaos. The old unconditional canonical Chaos texture selection discarded those permutations; the expanded resources preserve them.

The Moon page shows the regular and evolved bodies wearing a deep blue-purple textured finish. Its face and belly catch soft mauve/pink highlights over fine irregular facets/mottling. It is not a literal moon-shaped head, nor a pale lavender surface with a coarse grid of rectangular crater holes. Its ordinary pointed/crested anatomy still follows the relevant form. The page includes Moon versions of main, mixed secondary and Chaos forms. The new authored pattern borrows the finish's fine scale and colour family without copying a gallery image into the texture atlas.

Adult secondary development is gradual and reversible; it does not create a second cocoon. The diagram uses **Swim versus Fly** and **Run versus Power** axes around a Normal midpoint. The Neutral/Hero/Dark pages also show mixed secondary outcomes (Swim+Run, Swim+Power, Fly+Run and Fly+Power). Examples inspected include swept Neutral Run/Fly head blades, forked Hero Fly/Swim+Run crests, and the rearward red-striped Dark Run/Swim+Run form. Single discrete secondary labels are only a subset of that reference space; a resource expansion must not be described as reproducing every continuous interpolation.

The shiny-jewel PC/GC comparisons deliberately demonstrate version-specific colour and transparency outcomes, including bodies that become invisible while eyes/wings remain. Those images are not ordinary anatomy references and do not justify accidental missing limbs or opaque alpha rectangles. This release prioritizes coherent opaque coat permutations and continuous highlights instead of reproducing rendering bugs.

Animal-parts SA2 examples show slot-based mixtures and much more recognizable ears, paws, tails and wings than a generic stump. The three ghost-class animals do not introduce ordinary visible replacement sets. The SA1 page is kept as secondary reference for animals absent from SA2. A fully animal-covered Chao still follows the underlying Chao pose and ownership/heritage rather than becoming a new mob.

The hat tab shows head-covering novelty shells with visible eye holes, rather than all hats being small flat caps. Race medals are chest-mounted course emblems with distinct gemstone silhouettes. Drives are narrow crystals with metallic horizontal end bands and a dark central core. Tails, Knuckles and Amy are separate character forms. The Full-Egg and Omochao pages are explicitly under hacked Chao and are not evidence for normal breeding availability.

## Scope of implementation

The official comparison path keeps the supplied raw rig and texture selection separate from these authored inherited coats. Moon is a textured inherited finish, not a new alignment or literal lunar object. The 0.18 heritage finalizer generates native-UV Moon coats and genetic Light/Angel/Devil Chaos resources while preserving source alpha, eyes and aura regions. Texture coverage and state-to-resource mappings are tested separately from the bounded visual samples above.


## Heritage resource validation

`art/build_heritage_018.py` is the final writer after the generic official-coat importer and overlay retargeter. It authors 89 Moon resources and 696 expanded Chaos textures (348 genetic names across native and fitted-atlas layouts), plus six canonical-family resources. The generated manifest is `assets/chao_garden/heritage_coats_018.json`. The stock canonical family remains the ordinary two-tone non-shiny resource; the other inherited colour/tone/shiny/jewel combinations resolve to named files. There are 116 effective coat states per family, including normal and thirteen jewel/Moon finishes. The extra expanded normal-two-tone name is an intentional alias; ordinary canonical selection remains compatible with existing saves.

The generation run checks 720 skin-mask/alpha invariants and three eye-only paint operations. `art/validate_heritage_018.py` exhaustively checks 2,688 genetic state combinations and their 5,376 native/fitted texture paths, all 791 generated PNGs, the family eye texels, and exact byte equality of all twenty raw comparison PNGs with their supplied inputs. It passed 6,379 checks. Results are in [heritage-coats.json](../validation-0.18/heritage-coats.json). Moon egg longitude gutters match, and the fixed white crosses are not repainted. Actual client rendering is verified separately by the release owner; these asset checks do not replace that review.
