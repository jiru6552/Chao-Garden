## Approximations, failures and limits

The fixed Minecraft head deliberately does not reproduce the reference's changing rounded skull mass. The three-section horn and two distal lobes have blunt ends, fewer bends and shorter/shallow hooks than the smooth source. Front and above cameras hide some roots behind the box head or Dark marker. A specific young-tip readability check measured the actual emitted horn **1.2609444 model pixels above the head**, or **21.01574% of its six-pixel height**, in both default and production WALK120 joint frames. Its feature transform has no offset/rotation or scale suppression. The source cone is roughly a quarter-head height; the short blocky interpretation and occlusion remain disclosed approximations.

The source's conical forearms, spiral tail and curved bat wings are simplified into compact hands, broad stripe bands, a two-section tail and three closed rays per wing. Some cap/fork variants are subtle from the front and clearer from the side/back. Maturity and training use authored interpolation and young/half/mature color keys; the viewer's exact blend formulas and timing are unknown. A lower mature cap does not imply younger biological age. Genetic colors and finishes retain the form, but mono and jewel coats intentionally change accent contrast. Viewer lighting and camera/idle poses are not identical to Minecraft.
