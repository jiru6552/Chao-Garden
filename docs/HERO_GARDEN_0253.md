# Hero Garden: first playable foundation

The 0.25.3 Hero Garden is an independent playable realm, reached through the hub's upper balcony portal. Its first pass establishes the Battle-style asymmetric layout and care space. It is an authored Minecraft adaptation, not a recovered original mesh or a finished one-to-one replica.

## Reference and scale

[The directly inspected reference study](HERO_REFERENCE_0253.md) records the source images and hashes. The Battle overview determines the large pool on the left, broad lawn on the right, separate fountain temple and exit, pale classical pavilion, rock flanks, fallen column and distant bell island. The official Dreamcast pictures support the pink-cloud and pale-stone theme. The original Dreamcast river and bridge are deliberately excluded from this Battle-oriented first pass.

The lawn occupies an irregular superellipse within a 57 by 51 block box; its actual mask is smaller than that rectangle. This compact footprint is inferred from player movement and the reference's relative landmarks. No original game measurements have been recovered. Four palms leave a useful central care area. A low pink cloud sea and pale gold sky distinguish this garden from the Neutral Garden without enclosing it under a solid roof.

## Movement, care and persistence

The realm uses an empty terrain generator with its own daylight biome and client sky effect. Its biome has no precipitation, world features or wildlife spawn lists. The shared Chao World rules enforce the garden's Adventure care policy. The fixed transporter and native palm pods cannot be removed as purchased furniture. Bought care devices retain the existing garden placement rules.

The invisible cliff limit extends to the top of the realm, so Chao, eggs and dropped items cannot be thrown over a short wall into the cloud void. There is no visible ceiling. The decorative bell island lies beyond this playable edge.

Scene preparation preflights all writes. Existing purchased care blocks keep their states and block entities when the authored terrain leaves room for them; an obstructing change defers before any terrain mutation. A waterlogged bought toy requires source water to remain. Harvested native pods retain their growth stage. Existing keeper, Chao, egg and item identities are never recreated. Relocated bodies receive distinct collision-free dry lawn destinations; managed or busy Chao defer an unsafe rewrite. Late-loaded bodies trapped in authored rock are moved with the same identity, owner and state, and Chao registry locations record the Hero realm.

## Verification

`art/validate_hero_garden_0253.py --self-test` checks the native asset catalog, material bindings, opacity, east-facing emblem and isolated wildlife-free gold/pink sky. Its mutation check rejects a backwards emblem. The textures were also directly inspected as a three-material image.

`HeroGardenGeometryGameTests0253` checks actual ticking pond and waterfall source water, basin continuity, four complete pavilion columns, climbable stair orientation, the bell's real ceiling attachment, the open northern gap, absence of a visible roof, a collision-driven five-metre arrival walk, physical cliff containment and fixed native care providers. `HeroIntegrationGameTests0253` separately covers entry, return, care admission and persistent actor identity.

Native visual captures and automated run results are recorded by the release validation process. A source/asset check alone does not establish native visual approval.

## Remaining first-pass work

The garden is a playable foundation. Exact original contour matching, custom smooth pavilion geometry, additional reference-specific ornament, further plants and a polished environment soundscape remain later refinement. This pass does not add Hero alignment bonuses, new evolution rules, progression gates, reward eggs or mechanics inferred only from a screenshot.
