Chao Garden 0.25.16 — native-client review

Environment: Minecraft Java 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.219, Java 21,
1280x720 software-rendered desktop client, disposable synthetic save.
Plan: client-plan.json. Log: client-final.log. No user save modified.

Reviewed both native CWE item grids and all four equipped-clothing groups.
The source-derived fruits, brushes, gifts and instruments load with their
materials; item silhouettes and inventory icons remain recognizable.
All ten imported wearables were equipped on actual child Chao, including
blue and pink hoodies and mixed neck/face/head accessories.

Initial review caught rounded cloth intersecting the blockier Chao skin,
misoriented source rest poses, a disconnected headphone band and duplicate
reversed surfaces darkening/flickering in the no-cull renderer. Final meshes
correct the rest poses, split cloth at box-corner planes before projection,
join the source ear cups with a fitted band and emit each surface once.
The final hood is intentionally adapted to the blocky skull; it is not the
unmodified round CWE silhouette. Cap/press-hat crowns remain visible, eye
openings are clear, and the hood/sleeves follow their separate anchors.

Reviewed the Kindergarten exterior from the front and both roof obliques.
Both two-by-two windows sit inside the gable faces with solid backing,
aligned green sills and no roof masking their lower frames.

Reviewed representative Neutral/Hero/Dark views and the Kindergarten foyer,
classroom, principal room and clinic. Previous requested scenery reversions,
grass tops, source artwork and small garden banks remain intact.

Native probes also verified 26 source items in all nine display contexts,
128 placed toy states, 75 food/seed/tool items, 144 pod states, registered
blockstate materials and actual eating/planting/watering completion. The
27-scene run saved 30 scene captures, saved additional action frames and
restored the ordinary return location. See logs for exact capture hashes.

Scope: selected desktop-client scenes and child outfits were visually
inspected; independent geometric clearance covers 141 body cases. This is
not a claim of exhaustive animated/evolved-form, shader or Android testing.
