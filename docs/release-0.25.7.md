# Chao Garden 0.25.7

The Hero staircase rises in the opposite direction along the same lobby wall. Its upper gate and return landing move with the flight. The Dark staircase retains its route, with its gate's rigid flame model replaced by animated blue fire and flame particles.

The lobby's gate and stair scene appears on a fresh arrival while facing the garden gate, without first turning toward the central structure. The renderer submits the shared scene once per frame and retains the transparent surfaces' ordering.

The kindergarten entrance door leaves sit near the outside edge of their frame. The other ornate school doors sit near the frame edge facing the central lobby and open inward into their rooms. Their curved outlines, thin frames and paired interactions remain consistent with the new hinge positions. The hidden cabinet keeps its fitted carcass and front doors.

A small protected group of ambient baby Chao wanders and plays in the school zone. Classroom babies join the current lesson, including singing animations and effects. These ambient actors have their own saved identities and stay separate from the player's Chao, garden enrollment and paid school admissions.

Automatic music routing covers the Lobby, Neutral Garden, Hero Garden, Dark Garden, Kindergarten and Black Market. Neutral Garden reuses the existing supplied Sonic Marketplace recording; its exact SA2 OST rendition remains unverified. The five other original themes require supplied audio files; the companion resourcepack template and instructions are in [music-pack0257](music-pack0257/README.md). Areas without supplied tracks retain normal music behavior. The existing jukebox disc keeps its recording and ordinary controls.

Install the same `chao_garden-0.25.7.jar` on the server and every client, replacing the previous JAR. Minecraft Java 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219 and Java 21 remain required. Accessories and owo remain optional. See [TESTING.md](../TESTING.md) for final verification counts and scope.

Existing garden care, ownership and original-world return data are retained. Dark Garden remains a first pass, including its red-tinted native-water waterfall. Earlier supplied references remain in the complete project. Screenshot and video previews are omitted at the user's request.
