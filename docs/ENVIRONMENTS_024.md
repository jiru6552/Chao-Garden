# Chao lobby and Kindergarten scenery, 0.24

The hub follows the **neutral Sonic Adventure 2 lobby** in the supplied reference pictures: a broad round room, dark radial floor with gold rings, constellation panels, wooden trim, a cloudy upper dome, a gold and turquoise transporter, the blue-columned Kindergarten arch and the white Hero Garden stairs. The alternate Halloween and grass-floor examples are not mixed into this palette. The other garden doors are decorative placeholders; only Kindergarten and the return transporter travel in this release.

The Kindergarten has a continuous lobby and five connected rooms: classroom, health center, principal office, fortune house and Black Market. The bulletin board occupies the lobby wall. Doorways are three blocks wide, ceilings are eight blocks above the floor, and the four classroom student places stay clear. The physical service stations and residents use the same coordinate contract as the room builder.

The scenery uses vanilla blocks, stairs, slabs, signs, glass and lighting. It adds no decoration dependency. The requested Chao Teleporter is a two-block-high tube with an open entrance and center, four gold posts, translucent cyan side panes and luminous base/cap. Its item model shows the complete tube. The hub's large transporter encloses an actual working tube at its center.

`HubRooms.ensure` prepares a bounded plan after loading its chunks, applies only differing block states, and never clears entities. `HubContent` owns the persistent build revision and commits it only after a successful build. This prevents a normal visit from rebuilding scenery and permits future explicit scene revisions.

Art is reproducible with `scripts/generate_hub_assets_024.py`. The vinyl disc sprite is generated by `scripts/generate_music_assets.py`; its dark grooves, small colored label and open spindle hole follow Minecraft's music disc visual language.

The opt-in `-PchaoHub024Study=true -PchaoVisualTest=true` integrated study captures the actual game rather than a rendered design mockup. It uses controlled cameras and disposable test actors, while entering the tube and Kindergarten doorway, returning home, giving clothes, enrolling a student through the teacher button, opening the doctor tabs and starting/expiring finite flight through normal code. `scripts/build_preview_gallery_024.py` requires all planned captures and save acknowledgements before freezing the self-contained offline gallery.
