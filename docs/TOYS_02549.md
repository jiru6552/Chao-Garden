# Garden toys — Chao Garden 0.25.49

This update builds on the completed 0.25.48 source and preserves the piano, organ, guitar/accordion held poses, Analyzer, garden, Chaos Drive, equipped-flight, back-carry, running and lobby work. Java 21, Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219 and external Lionfish API 3.1 remain the build/runtime requirements. Use matching client and server Chao Garden JARs.

## Source and adaptation

See [the reference inventory](TOY_REFERENCES_02549.md) for exact source addresses, hashes and reference links. Original supplied SA2 Battle geometry is used for the horse, car, Sonic doll, broom, book, crayons and pogo stick. Shadow uses the extracted Sonic plush geometry adapted with official Shadow texture information and an authored black/red/white palette; a verified independent Shadow geometry root was not found. The previously imported guitar and accordion came from the supplied CWE assets; the existing triangle and beater are authored mod geometry.

CWE source motion names and approach code provide behavioral context. The retained video research does not contain verified footage of these toy actions. These Lionfish curves, Minecraft positioning, 160-tick phrases and sound choices are authored adaptations, not direct imports of original game animation curves. No claim of exact original-game timing or listening is made.

All activities use the existing natural selection, reservation, reward, cooldown and source-recovery system. New toys do not introduce lessons or alter care/genetics. Hand presentations hide their one reserved source and never mint an inventory copy. Entry/exit and interruption share production clocks with operator previews. Collision-checked mounted recovery preserves original NoAI/NoGravity flags, including deferred rescue when no supported exit is available. Low-profile floor toys use a short outside-cell approach; its pace now stays above vanilla's movement cutoff for an untrained crawling baby, without changing Run points or movement attributes.

## Coffin migration

[Detailed compatibility behavior](COFFIN_RETIREMENT_02549.md): existing placed coffins become a small neutral stone marker with no collision, toy behavior or loot. Existing stored stacks become inert paper-icon legacy tokens, preserving their counts, names, custom data and nested components. They cannot be placed or used as a toy and are hidden from creative inventory. Markers can be removed manually. No automatic inventory/chunk sweep deletes or rewrites unrelated data. Saved merchant offers are revalidated against the current catalog; the retired reward flag is harmless historical data.

The old registry IDs and blockstate property names are deliberately retained to decode saves safely. Administrative commands may still resolve those compatibility IDs; they do not create a usable coffin. Unloaded chunks/stored inventories remain untouched until normally loaded. Old active mounted sessions cancel safely on reload. No bulk save conversion or downgrade guarantee is claimed.

## Previews

Operator commands use the actual production interaction, no rewards and no permanent changes:

```text
/chao testtoy list
/chao testtoy rocking_horse once
/chao testtoy toy_car loop
/chao testtoy sonic_doll once
/chao testtoy shadow_doll once
/chao testtoy broom once
/chao testtoy picture_book once
/chao testtoy pogo_stick loop
/chao testtoy crayons once
/chao testtoy stop
```

Every listed toy also accepts its named `once`, `loop` and `stop`. Horse and pogo previews require the matching placed toy nearby, supported ground and clear riding space. Other new previews need no inventory toy or lesson. Saved care, age, genes, lessons, real items, cooldown and original AI/gravity flags are preserved. Damage, care interaction, movement, toy removal, unloading, logout, range and stop clean up. A sealed ride cannot be replaced by another preview that overwrites its recovery flags.

## Verification

Initial common compilation and the core mechanics suite passed. All 407 native dedicated GameTest checks passed with real Accessories/owo loaded: 222 new toy/mount/preview checks, eight coffin compatibility checks and 177 existing regressions. This includes both ages and four orientations for natural play, lesson behavior on existing instruments, original-state preservation, source removal, reload, blocked riding space, rescue, and five existing lifesaver checks.

An additional 41 native checks passed for natural use of all five flattened toys with untrained Run-0 babies and adults in all four orientations, plus actual server RecipeManager loading of all seven new recipes. The total is **448 passing native checks**. The tests caught and helped fix the crawling approach cutoff and selection of a blocked horse side. A sealed-pogo test fixture was corrected to block a genuine small exit without automatically boarding the Chao in its collision-blocking boat.

All **60 actual Minecraft client cases** passed: 20 floor-toy/orientation cases and 40 activity sessions (five per activity: natural baby, natural adult, replacement-hand preview, Chaos looping preview, and a placed-toy interruption). The final horse foot refinement was rechecked in five additional sessions. Captures require an actual matching rendered Chao/world, without menus, overlays or shadow passes. Natural cases travel to the toy; previews check exact original state and AI/gravity restoration; cues and cessation are checked as real client sound events. Multiple entry, contact, action, page/ink/bellows/rock/hop and exit frames were visually inspected. These sessions use the normal production tick rate, not a slowed demonstration.

Source/rig checks passed privately and again in the release build: horse 38,622 poses / 32,028 emitted hands; car/dolls/pogo 30,510 poses / 44,748 emitted hands plus actual replacement/glass-foot pedal fitting; broom/book/crayons 59,346 poses / 96,084 emitted hands. Each group passed 641 quarter-tick clock samples. Arms stay within 3.2 model pixels of extension. Actual hand/head/body and prop/head/body checks remain positive across the tested source forms, age scales, nine morph combinations and replacement hands. The 28 focused floor/source/audio asset checks passed, including unchanged source surfaces/UVs, correct world grounding and independent source-contact containment. All 46 protected pre-existing held/item assets are unchanged, and the triangle inventory display is an exact copy of the previous definition.

Automated geometry assertions are not visual or listening acceptance. The installed runtime for the client/native studies includes real Accessories/owo. Shaders were disabled in these toy studies; the existing lobby/shader implementation is unchanged and is compared with 0.25.48 during packaging.

Production-JAR integrity and checksums are recorded in the delivery evidence. `build -x triangleTransitionRigTest` passed the new toy checks and existing mechanics/rig checks, then stopped at the unchanged trumpet transition test. The remaining `visibleTransitionRigTest assemble` invocation passed, including all 264 visible-transition checks and the production JAR. The full build is therefore **not green**: two inherited transition-test failures remain, without relaxing either test or changing unrelated instrument poses.

## Remaining manual acceptance

Listen to the new car, doll, sweep, paper, spring and crayon sounds in game, including multiple Chao. Check each toy with your preferred variants and modpack, and compare the authored adaptations with any specific original-game footage you prefer. Independent multiplayer/network-latency and full-modpack tests have not yet been performed in this update. Cloud captures use Mesa software rendering. The environment has no `/dev/snd` hardware device; the client used a silent OpenAL backend. New sound assets decode and their dispatched timings/counts pass, but tone, loudness and listening were not verified. No separate networked players or full modpack were tested.
