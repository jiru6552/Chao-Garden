# Chao Garden 0.25.1

This update refines Chao World against the two supplied SA2/Battle recordings and the 25 in-game/reference screenshots. The [current gallery](release-gallery-0.25.1.html) documents the new geometry, materials, interactions and portal motion. Final validation results and scope are recorded in [TESTING.md](../TESTING.md).

The first Neutral Garden now follows the source layout: the waterfall and fully filled pond lie west of the compact cave exit, and the transporter sits on the eastern grassy ridge. The cave is cut into a continuous rock wall without the former oversized roof/notch or wooden sign. The recess behind the waterfall is sealed. Invisible barriers protect the open cliff edge. Palm and flower placement checks actual dry ground, and the rock crests use thin grass edges. The scenery upgrade preserves existing Chao identities and care props, clears obsolete authored fruit/transporter locations, and moves bodies that would otherwise be buried or newly submerged by the reshaped terrain. This also covers bodies loaded after the scenery upgrade. See [garden notes](GARDEN_REFINEMENT_0251.md).

Registered gardens retain their owners, names and members when the old authored transporter moves. A real replacement provider is placed before registration changes; occupied destinations use a separate nearby provider. Saved Garden Bells follow validated relocation records after reload. Physical last-seen locations remain unchanged until the corresponding body actually moves, and removing the old provider does not delete the relocated registration. Garden Bells use their normal linking and assignment interactions in Adventure mode.

The lobby and return gates have two-sided animated End portal surfaces inside closed quartz frames. The garden arch has radial stones, pale spiral columns, urns, flowers and small Chao statues. Kindergarten gate roof undersides are closed. The central exit has separated readable lettering, a closed upper assembly and corrected portal depth; the surrounding clouds blend across their former hard boundary and dome poles. Travel checks use the same aperture geometry as the visible portals. Present destinations prepare before the first lobby arrival, so crossing a gate does not begin a heavy scenery build while the client continues moving. See [portal notes](PORTALS_0251.md).

The school has working paired doors with mirrored leaves, complete frames and ordinary right-click opening/closing. Each complete pair predicts together on the client; ordinary server acknowledgements reconcile rejected clicks without leaving partial door states. Its entrance title is centered and the gold/green Chao emblem is complete. Invisible boundaries keep visitors on the public approach, including the porch, the rear of the return gate and the air above the hedges and gable. The enclosure preserves existing hedges, pillars, roof and portal frames. Wall-mounted artwork, full-height pillars, fitted windows, wall-reaching curtains, complete book backs and decoration faces replace the exposed gaps, clipping and stretched surfaces. The Black Market uses a storage-closet entrance leading to its resident room. The classroom has mountain scenery on the walls and clouds on the ceiling. The Fortune-Teller's table screen blocks bodies while leaving the actual interaction ray open. See [school notes](SCHOOL_REFINEMENTS_0251.md).

The Fortune-Teller's authored closed lids remain fixed during both idle time and service gestures. The Doctor's tooth-like grin is replaced by a swept gray moustache. Other staff retain their existing profession models and adult presentation. See [staff notes](STAFF_0251.md).

All three realms retain **Adventure mode**. Normal Chao release, eggs, seeds, toys and activities remain available through their native item behavior. To recover a placed garden prop, sneak-right-click it with both hands empty. Authored terrain and palms remain protected. Permission for a care-item action is scoped to that synchronous native action; no build ability or extra Adventure item component is left behind.

Outdoor realm biomes have empty wildlife spawn lists. A realm-specific admission check also prevents passive or hostile mobs from entering old saved chunks and clears previously spawned wildlife, while allowing Chao, Kindergarten staff and the resident merchant. Ordinary-world spawning rules are preserved. Returning home chooses clear ground beside the original teleporter; its roof is excluded as a landing surface. Original location, orientation and game mode continue to use the persistent return journal.

## Source and preview retention

The complete download contains current editable code, native resources, the supplied screenshot/reference evidence, current captures and validation reports. Its gallery refers to media within the extracted archive. The separate standalone gallery embeds its required media for viewing without sidecars or a network connection. The older embedded 0.25 gallery remains in the previous saved release; its native baby clips and source provenance are retained and labeled historical, rather than presented as fresh 0.25.1 captures.

The rooms and terrain remain authored Minecraft adaptations of the supplied recordings. Hero and Dark garden gates remain reserved. Existing acquisition rules, Chao care, evolution, breeding, competition and companion systems continue from the prior release.
