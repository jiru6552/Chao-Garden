# Chao themes — 0.23.0

The retained collection has **nineteen themes**: seventeen mod tributes plus Nether and End. Models, coats, egg art, registry IDs and saved heritage remain available in the source. New acquisition of the seventeen mod-addon forms is paused for this phase, even when their source mod is installed. Nether and End eggs instead have rare loot sources in their respective dimensions.

No egg crafting conversion is active. The old twenty egg recipes and nineteen themed recipe unlocks are removed. The legacy themed recipe serializer remains registered so older data can be read, but it cannot match or assemble an egg. The materials in the table are retained design history and do not craft a Chao.

## New creatures and saved worlds

New addon eggs cannot be used or hatched. Eggs already in the world remain recoverable and preserve their incubation and individual data during the pause. The creative catalog and the mod’s themed-egg command exclude paused forms. New offspring and new Omochao cannot copy paused addon heritage; their ordinary coat genes remain. Existing living Chao keep their saved heritage, and existing reincarnation data is preserved.

Appearance availability is separate from acquisition. An existing mod-themed Chao shows its theme while a matching source-mod ID is loaded, and becomes visually dormant when that mod is absent. These are visual tributes; they do not implement the source mod’s gameplay. The Neo Vitae display/command name remains `neovitae`; its earlier serialized `blood_magic` identity is retained for saved-world compatibility.

`/chao variants` lists currently obtainable themes. The operator command `/chao variantegg <id>` admits Nether and End and rejects paused addon forms. Commands are administrative tools; regular survival acquisition follows [the distribution rules](release-0.23.0.md).

The twenty-nine themes retired in earlier releases remain a different category: their compatibility egg entries load as ordinary forms. See [retirement handling](THEME_RETIREMENT_014.md).
