frown, grin and wavy. Assignment hashes the existing thirteen diploid personality gene pairs, expressed

## Temporary expressions

The production resolver evaluates both channels once after ordinary pose/action sampling. Native artwork is
excluded first. Joint overrides then prefer **damage → danger/flailing → sleep/rest → active illness symptom →
illness**. Cough, sneeze and hiccup use squeezed distress eyes/open mouth; scratch, stomach ache and runny
nose share half-closed eyes/wavy mouth. Sharing reactions is deliberate; six uniquely verified original
illness faces are not claimed.

Otherwise activity happiness and affection precede grogginess, authored upset behavior and these
existing-meter thresholds, in the listed order:

| Condition | Temporary eyes / mouth |
| --- | --- |
| Fear ≥115 | Surprised / open |
| Cry ≥100 | Distress / frown |
| Anger ≥105 | Mean / frown |
| Sleepiness or tire ≥8000; tiredness ≥6000; or energy ≤15 | Half-closed / wavy |
| Joy ≥115 | Happy-closed / smile |
| Relax ≥70 | Half-closed / smile |
| Lonely ≥4000 | Half-closed / frown |

Lower-priority behavior can reserve the mouth separately; eating and blowing retain their action mouth. Those
mouth reservations do not supersede damage, sleep or illness. Production blinking replaces eligible
baseline/mood eyes without replacing the mouth. When temporary inputs clear, the resolver returns to the saved
baseline. Thresholds and this precedence are authored presentation rules, not newly established original-game
mechanics.

## Operator previews

```text
/chao testface happy once
/chao testface sleep loop
/chao testface sleep stop
/chao testface cough once
/chao testface stop
/chao testface list
/chao testtoy face_happy once
/chao testtoy list
```

Available IDs are `happy`, `distress`, `tired`, `sleep`, `cough`, `cold`, `rash`, `runny_nose`, `hiccups` and
`stomach_ache`; every ID supports once/loop/stop. Permission level 2 and an eligible ordinary Chao within 12
blocks on dry, supported ground are required. Native Chaos and character previews are rejected. These commands
reuse the existing `ToyPreview` pipeline and production resolver. A once session lasts 160 ticks, with its
presentation token active at ticks 20–139. No actual mood, care, illness or identity is edited and no
reward/item consumption is introduced. Existing preview cleanup restores temporary AI and stops on explicit
stop, interaction, damage, unload, disconnect, dimension/range loss or reload. Real damage still applies.
Native dispatch and the selected real-client preview/recovery scenes passed.
