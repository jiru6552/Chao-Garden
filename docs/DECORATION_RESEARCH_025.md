# Decoration research — 0.25

The two supplied SA2 recordings are the primary spatial and animation references. The refreshed rooms use authored native blocks so their proportions, palettes and furniture can follow those recordings. None of the following mods is a required dependency, and no decoration-mod artwork is included.

| Project | Verified NeoForge / Minecraft 1.21.1 release | Useful reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Handcrafted](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/handcrafted/files/all) | 4.0.3, 22 March 2025 | Chairs, desks, tables, shelves and storage suggest the scale and usability of walkable furniture. |
| [Chipped](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/chipped/files/all) | 4.0.2, 15 October 2024 | Its glass, wood and stone material variety informed the decision to give the school dedicated architectural materials. |
| [Supplementaries](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/supplementaries/files/all) | 1.21.1-4.0.2, 16 October 2026 | Globes, jars and candle holders suit the Principal and Fortune-Teller rooms. |

Publisher pages were checked on 1 November 2026. These are confirmed compatible releases, not a claim that every combination has been tested with this mod. The projects' client/server installation requirements also make them unsuitable as an invisible server-only scenery dependency.

The native set provides the school’s arched labels, blue facade, oval windows, twisted columns, teal foyer rug, wall drawings, furniture, curtains, medical fittings, globe, crystal ball and classroom toys. A separate scene renderer supplies the lobby’s transparent disk, radial grid, gold rings and cloudy view below. These pieces avoid substituting generic furniture where the reference has a distinctive silhouette.
