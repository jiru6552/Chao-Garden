# Chao Garden 0.25.10

For Minecraft Java 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.219 / Java 21. Replace the previous Chao Garden JAR on both client and server.

- **Consolidated fruit:** Hero Garden Fruit and Dark Garden Fruit now move a child's affinity toward Hero/Dark by 0.15 after a completed meal, while keeping their +40 Stamina benefit in both food profiles. Adult affinity stays locked. Separate Hero Fruit and Dark Fruit no longer appear as distinct items, recipes, trades or trees.
- **Existing saves:** old fruit stacks and seeds resolve to the garden variants, retaining counts and stack components. Existing pods, crowns and seedlings resolve with their growth properties intact. The same market offer slots now contain the garden variants. There are 35 active foods and 33 active seed families.
- **Hero pool:** repaired the missing marble rim cells and closed the other exposed gaps in the same perimeter rule.
- **First garden:** compared the Neutral Garden against retained frames from the supplied SA2 Battle recording. Added paler cascade limestone and nine broad-leaf plants, lightened the water, and grounded the affected flower clusters. The requested layout, palms, pond footprint and compact cave remain.

Authored-room revision 35 applies scenery updates through the existing room migration path, retaining its care, ownership and return-location protections. The scenic water tint also applies to kindergarten decorative water because it uses the same biome.
