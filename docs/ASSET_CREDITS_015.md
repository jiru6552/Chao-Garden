# Asset provenance — 0.15

The Chao base geometries, texture maps and animation definitions are converted from the Sonic the Hedgehog Marketplace world by Gamemode One supplied by the project owner. Sonic/Chao assets are attributed to SEGA / Sonic Team and Gamemode One. The minimal reproducible art inputs are under `art/imported_chao`; the conversion pipeline is `art/import_official_chao.py`. The mod runs the converted geometry and animation curves through its own NeoForge rendering code.

Custom mod-themed coats, badges, goggles and crowns, animal-gene parts, Chaos-form adaptations, UI, orchard items, transporter block and competition content are project work. Those additions are retargeted to the imported rig. The supplied world contains no Chao egg or fruit assets, so egg corrections and SA2 fruit/transporter art are authored separately.

The Chao Garden CD combines the supplied Neutral Garden intro and loop recordings, attributed to SEGA / Sonic Team. The existing five Chao voice recordings retain the provenance in [AUDIO_CREDITS.md](AUDIO_CREDITS.md). The source Marketplace world is not bundled in full; account files, entitlement caches and decryption keys are absent from the project and release.

Visual/gameplay references: [SA2 fruit catalog](https://chao-island.com/wiki/Fruit), [garden fruit variants](https://chao-island.com/wiki/Garden_Fruit), [Black Market fruit](https://chao-island.com/info-center/kindergarten/black-market.html), and [PC transporter](https://chao-island.com/info-center/misc/chao-transporter.html). Natural Overworld discovery and growing market fruit after buying it are Minecraft adaptations requested for this mod.
