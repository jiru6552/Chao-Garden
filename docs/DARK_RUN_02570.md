## Approximations, failures and limits

This is a complete first pass of the nine-target family, with explicit geometric approximations. The fixed box head and arms obscure some rear-quill roots and wing area from front and underside cameras. The two-section tips are blunt, and several downward side ends stop shorter/higher than the smooth source hooks. Three closed rays per wing remain more segmented and shallower than the reference's broad curved membranes. Single-block nubs approximate rounded/tapered lobes. The source's changing rounded skull mass is deliberately not reproduced. Linear shape development, bilinear training and staged color keys approximate the observed progression; they are not extracted source formulas.

The initial actual focused check caught insufficient fan exposure beside the broader Power torso: the worst mature margin was 0.41261554 model pixels against the existing 0.7 minimum; half Power+Swim also failed. A short actual emitted-placement diagnostic identified those two targets. Only their authored spans changed, from 2.50→2.95 and 2.65→3.00. The torso, head, hinges and other seven mature targets stayed unchanged. A new-family directional fixture now compares compact Power with the clearly broader Fly source fan, rather than asserting an unsupported exact Power-versus-balanced ranking from occluded torso pixels. The original 54-case 0.7 visibility guard and all collision, UV, attachment and continuity limits remain intact. The corrected actual diagnostic and complete affected suite passed. Earlier failed inputs/logs are preserved separately and are not final evidence.

The 31-scene actual preflight passed and revealed no blocking visible defect in the reviewed samples. Root personally opened 13 sheets representing 144 unique originals; a supplementary model review covered 140 unique originals. Hats intentionally hide natural head features, and rabbit/Phoenix parts replace their corresponding natural parts. Player carriage is substantially occluded from the front; side/back views are the useful attachment checks. Crawl/glide and floor-play folding hide some fan surfaces behind the torso. The evolution cocoon obscures the precise emergence shape, so direct newly evolved views provide the useful shape evidence.

The separate actual journal-content check remains **8/9** because of the inherited `Your gardens` paragraph-separator issue. The checker and journal content are byte-identical to completed 0.25.69; this is separate from the morphology checks. The shared floor-palm envelope limitation documented in 0.25.69 was not redesigned by this model update. Held-grip targets and wing clearance have direct checks, but floor/prop surfaces still obscure some palm edges; check their appearance on the phone.

Timed stills and bounded decoded movie samples do not establish continuous playback or listening. The viewer does not verify genetic colors or shininess; canonical-coat tests and representative actual game finishes cover those separately. No physical phone/touch test, shader-pack sweep, exhaustive toy/attachment matrix, independent remote-player session, full pre-update-world restart or complete natural lifetime was tested. Native entity/tag roundtrips, tracked-observer checks and integrated-client updates have narrower scope than those scenarios. Creative controls are exercised through their mouse handlers; this does not establish phone usability. Graphical checks use the real development-client classpath. The final runtime proof must bind its production bytes to the delivered JAR; `runClient` is not claimed to have loaded that archive.

## Delivery and suggested checks

Use matching client/server JARs with Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219, Java 21 and the existing external Lionfish API 3.1. The source ZIP is a cumulative overlay onto complete 0.25.43 source retaining completed local 0.25.69. Inherited source-only guarded deletions do not touch saves.

For an already evolved full-Dark (−100), Run-first adult, the existing test commands can develop Power+Swim without changing first Run:

```mcfunction
/chao ~ -100 Run 0
/chao ~ -100 Power 10
/chao ~ -100 Fly 0
/chao ~ -100 Swim 10
```

Quoted names also work, for example `/chao "Shade" -100 Power 10`. A partly Dark adult requires its existing locked alignment value; adult alignment edits are rejected. The Analyzer maturity bar adjusts current-stage development without resetting lifespan or first evolution.

Existing operator previews include `/chao testpose crawl loop`, `/chao testpose swim once`, `/chao testpose stop`, `/chao testtoy broom once`, `/chao testtoy picture_book loop`, `/chao testtoy toy_car once`, `/chao testtoy crayons loop`, `/chao testtoy stop` and `/chao testcarry loop|stop`. They reuse production clocks and cleanup, preserve care/age/real items, grant no rewards, restore temporary AI flags and stop on interaction, damage, unload, disconnect or explicit stop.
