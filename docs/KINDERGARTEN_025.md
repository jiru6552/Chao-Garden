# Kindergarten 0.25: supplied-video reconstruction

The primary reference is the owner's complete 299.308-second Kindergarten recording, `SONIC ADVENTURE 2 2026-11-01 13-45-09.mp4`. Native 2560×1440 frames, the complete five-second contact sheets and denser market samples were reviewed. This update uses authored Minecraft block geometry and textures, with separate professional adult Chao models; the owner can walk through the connected rooms before choosing a service.

The supplied recording shows no original classroom teacher. The teacher's academic cap, vest and book are an intentional addition requested by the owner. All actual staff are adult cyan Chao with profession-specific anatomy/accessories; their ordinary coat and ability points are not abused to simulate adulthood or a costume.

## In-scene service presentation

The new service screen leaves the 3D room visible. A role-colored header, compact right-side actions and a bottom dialogue strip replace the old opaque blue journal. Only interaction with a resident or the physical noticeboard opens it. Walking through an open arch never opens a UI. The medical chart and name editor appear as small overlays, and keyboard focus/narration use native Minecraft widgets. Layout coordinates scale together with pointer input.

The existing server session nonce, real resident identity, distance, keeper ownership, cursor/menu state, read/write cooldowns, classroom tickets and saved student ledger remain authoritative. Manual selection has its own four-tick read budget, separate from the teacher heartbeat, so a background refresh cannot swallow an intentional choice. Names are sanitized and bounded by the existing server rules. A classroom heartbeat refreshes the visible countdown without consuming the cooldown for care transactions. The chart reads the selected Chao's actual state, including grades, levels, points, personality, health and earned records.

## Black Market buyback is a Minecraft adaptation

At approximately 04:33.75 the player selects **Sell items**. The merchant says only that the player has nothing to sell and should bring eligible items. There is no demonstrated sale, item eligibility list, completed transaction or buyback rate. Therefore the video does **not** establish original sale prices.

This mod's explicit adaptation quotes **one quarter of the existing catalog purchase value for the complete inventory lot, rounded down once**. A one-emerald item needs at least four items before the lot earns one emerald. There is no minimum one-emerald payout per item. No source claims this is an original SA2/CWE rate.

Only plain, default-component `chao_garden` items in the real Black Market catalog are considered. Eggs, Chao carriers, robot components/hearts, items with custom data/names/damage/enchantments, unrecognized items and every currently loaded recipe output are rejected. Recipe output exclusions are rebuilt on each quote/commit so datapack reloads do not leave a stale crafting/arbitrage allowance. The client supplies an inventory slot and a one-use quote token; neither its item, amount, price nor currency is trusted.

Confirmation rechecks the real item, components, count and catalog quote. It plans the complete 36-slot inventory before changing anything, including all payment stacks. Up to 64 emeralds are paid as plain emeralds; larger payments use emerald blocks plus a remainder. If the complete payment cannot fit, the goods and money remain unchanged and the quote can be retried. A successful commit consumes the quote before publishing only the changed inventory slots. Payment uses no entity spawn, drop or external insertion callback, so denied drops cannot lose or replay a sale. Buying and stock limits remain the existing merchant implementation.

## World and integration contract

`KindergartenRooms025.ensure` applies only inside the Kindergarten dimension and within `(-37,76,-34)` to `(38,103,52)`. The courtyard entry is `(0.5,80.1,40.5)`. The school front door is around `(0,80,18)`; it leads physically into the foyer. The separate courtyard return arch occupies X−2..2, Z47..50. Existing teacher, doctor, principal, fortune, market and student-seat anchors remain stable for saved identities and classroom records. The doctor and fortune-teller sit 0.4 blocks above the floor; the principal uses the existing 0.5 lift.

`KindergartenDecor025` registers the native scenery and its BlockItems. The generator `scripts/generate_kindergarten_assets_025.py` emits the manifest, models, textures, blockstates, block drops and an independent locale fragment. Integration reads `assets/chao_garden/kindergarten_decor025.json` rather than a hardcoded block count. This module creates no decoration-mod dependency. Handcrafted, Chipped and Supplementaries were considered for furniture; authored native pieces better match the distinctive video furniture, painted doorways and toy town while preserving a small compatible installation.

The final layout uses a 15-by-19-block chamfered foyer and five-block clear interior height. The connected services are arranged around the preserved residents: classroom X−25..−13/Z−22..−8, clinic X13..25/Z−21..−9, principal X−5..5/Z−25..−16, fortune X18..26/Z0..10 and market X−25..−17/Z1..9. Narrow connecting passages replace the first diagnostic build's oversized open floor areas. The physical noticeboard is at X−5/Y82/Z8; its invisible protected service anchor remains at the original location.

There are 79 native scenery registry IDs. Two bounded atlas blocks avoid creating one inventory item for every painted tile: the composed rug uses 117 tiles (9×13), and the large cloud mural uses 16 tiles (4×4). Both have native FACING plus TILE 0..127 state domains; unused tile states fall back to tile zero. Each item shows its tile-zero model. Cloud scenery emits light to retain the reference classroom's bright appearance after applying its mural ceiling.

Small tabletop props were reduced against the actual adult head width, and their native geometry rests on the ten-pixel counter surface. The principal desk and fortune altar are each authored as one 1.5-block-wide piece with their books/papers/quill/globe or crystal/candles/bottles composed directly on the tabletop, avoiding separate floating pieces. The crystal ball's diameter is approximately half the adult head width. The doctor, principal and fortune-teller use native seated poses, with chair seats matched to those poses. Resident body/head facing is fixed on both client and server; this avoids a sideways/backwards fortune-teller when ordinary mob body control is deliberately paused.

The gameplay HUD and resident name tags are suppressed only while the production Kindergarten conversation overlay is active. The 3D room, held patient and hand rendering remain visible. No global HUD option is changed. Empty decorative arch/panel/leaf/window collision remains passable for navigation; solid partial furniture is treated as a land obstacle. The long exam bed's collision follows its two-block model in every facing. All placed vanilla hedge leaves are persistent, so the school landscaping does not decay in ordinary play.

## Validation

The first real-client diagnostic views exposed oversized volumes, a hovering roof, repeated tiny floor/cloud motifs, oversized tabletop props and seated staff occlusion. The compact diagnostic revealed a separate architectural union bug: corridor end caps were retained inside the foyer and service rooms. Clearing the full room/foyer interiors after constructing passage boundaries removes those real obstructions; merely moving a camera would have concealed them. A real full-size player movement test walks all six public routes out and back through vanilla collision resolution, checking support and body clearance at every step. Actual service selection and direct vanilla hedge random-tick regressions accompany the four real buyback transaction tests. Those findings drove the compact reconstruction described above. The new composed-atlas resource contract verifies finite registry IDs, every facing/tile state, all 117+16 model closures, fallback states and UV bounds. A direct resource check also rejects out-of-range native model elements, inverted elements, unresolved textures and missing item/blockstate files. Fresh compile, core tests, actual server buyback tests and real-client captures are run by the shared project toolchain; their final results belong to the release validation report, not to the historical 0.24 counts.
