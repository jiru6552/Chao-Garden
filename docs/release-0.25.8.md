# Chao Garden 0.25.8

Dark Garden fruit trees are one block taller so their hanging fruit is one block higher. Existing authored trees update with the room revision while the protected garden-care and owned-object rules remain intact.

All four ambient baby Chao remain inside the classroom. Two can wander and play with the classroom toys; the other two join the current class. Their saved identities stay separate from player-owned Chao and the four paid student places. The previous lobby babies return to the classroom when the updated school is entered.

Song lessons use original pitched Chao-vocal phrases with notes and rests, derived from the supplied voice timbre. The ambient pupils follow the actual current lesson. These newly arranged phrases are an adaptation; no original SA2 singing recording was supplied.

The classroom play city uses rebuilt, separated house, tower and tree geometry, with space around the teacher desk and pupil activities. The ornate school doors open in the opposite direction from 0.25.7 and close against their frames without the visible side slit. The entrance leaves remain at the outside frame edge, while internal leaves remain at the lobby-facing edge. The hidden tool cabinet retains its independent design and behavior.

Music routing still covers the Lobby, Neutral Garden, Hero Garden, Dark Garden, Kindergarten and Black Market. Neutral Garden reuses the supplied Sonic Marketplace recording, whose exact SA2 OST rendition remains unverified. Five other original themes still need supplied audio files; the companion resourcepack template is in [music-pack0257](music-pack0257/README.md).

Install the same `chao_garden-0.25.8.jar` on the server and every client, replacing the previous JAR. Minecraft Java 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.219 and Java 21 remain required. Accessories and owo are optional. See [TESTING.md](../TESTING.md) for completed verification counts and scope.

The flipped Hero staircase, animated Dark gate fire, fresh-arrival scene fix, existing care and ownership data, and original-world returns remain included. Original reference inputs are retained. Screenshot and video previews are omitted at the user's request.
