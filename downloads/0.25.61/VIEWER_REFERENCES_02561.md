## Anatomy and natural rendered colors

At viewer age0 all six stat combinations visibly converge. The head/body are pale pink/lilac. Two thick upper-lateral crests sweep outward/back, with small downward-bent dark-blue terminal points; there is no central Normal stem. Their roots lie toward the upper rear of the head, and a side view foreshortens them into compact curls. Wings are large green/cyan fans with roughly three pointed rays/lobes per side. A rear tail bends upward to a blue point. Arms show lilac/purple bands into blue ends; the chest patch and feet are peach/gold. The emotion marker remains a **smooth round peach/salmon orb**, neither a halo nor a spiked Dark marker.

## Age and training

The same six forms are captured at ages0/.25/.5/.75/1. Front/side/back are present at0/.5/1; mature forms add opposite side/above/below. All six age-progression sheets and the three front/side/back sheets were personally opened. Age drives continuous crest direction/length, wing size and palette changes. Balanced crests remain compact at young ages and extend rear/up strongly later; Power's tall hooked crests also develop progressively. Run curls thicken, while pure Fly wings expand strongly and transition from green/cyan toward white/yellow. Paired forms stay visually distinct rather than following a simple average of pure endpoint silhouettes.

Fixed mature-age training25/50/75% samples are also captured independently from age and the completed sheet was personally inspected. Single Run, Power and Fly controls change gradually across the range. Both compatible paired controls reach a nearly unchanged combined endpoint by about50% on each signed axis: their actual full-canvas UI thumb positions were checked at the halfway points, while50/75/100% look very similar. A linear/bilinear mod interior can therefore provide a clear smooth first pass, but its interior mixture/rate must be labeled an approximation rather than an exact copy of the viewer blending. Viewer slider progression is a visual guide; it does not define the mod's biological timing, bars, tie recency, identity locking, permissions or gameplay stat gain/loss rules.

## Limits

Rendered colors are visual estimates under viewer lighting. Genetic colors, shininess, mod motion, native-client appearance and gameplay rules require separate production checks. Control percentages are approximate. A few blocky segments and smooth endpoint interpolation will be an explicit first pass approximation to this rounded reference; the reference is not proof of exact shape, blend function or original-game timing.
