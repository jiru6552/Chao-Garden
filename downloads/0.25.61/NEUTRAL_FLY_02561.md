# Neutral Fly-first morphology — 0.25.61

This update adds the ordinary **Neutral / Fly-first** adult family: balanced, Run, Power, Fly, Fly+Run and Fly+Power. Pair order selects the same shared form. Swim counters later Fly toward balanced Fly-first; excess Swim does not add a Swim secondary form or change the locked first type. This family is distinct from a Neutral/Normal-first adult's later Fly specialization.

The original blocky 7×6×6 skull, pixel eyes, expression geometry, unspiked marker and 59-bone hierarchy remain in place. Seventeen closed target boxes add four paired crest sections, five compact body/hand/foot boxes, six wing rays and two tail sections. The existing Lionfish renderer and ten-target rigid-box evaluator serve visible surfaces and attachment sampling. Logical identity stays `fly`, with the canonical baby UV and attachment family. No new dependency, general morphology API or viewer geometry/code is imported.

## Development and compatibility

Selection requires a living ordinary mortal adult, normal character/variant, locked Neutral alignment strictly between -0.5 and +0.5, and locked first type Fly. Other families retain their existing selection rules.

Later Run/Power influence is `(Power - Run) / 960`, clamped to -1..1. Swim/Fly is `(Fly - Swim) / 960`, clamped to 0..1. Each value comes from the current `100 × level + progress`, separate from earned points and recency used for first evolution. Ordinary/combined drives, decreases and Creative edits therefore affect the same current target. Equal opposing bars restore balance; additional Swim leaves later Fly at zero while Run/Power remains available.

Maturity independently blends the common fresh surface into the mature lattice. Twenty-tick display easing smooths large changes. Baby training carries forward, and stat reductions can recede specialization without reducing biological age or resetting stats. The **960-net-progress threshold is provisional mod pacing**, not viewer timing. Compatible axes use bilinear weights; the viewer's pairs appear nearly at their combined endpoint by half training, so the interior blend/rate is an explicit approximation. Full passive visual growth still requires **14.4 active hours** with the default clock, against the ordinary twelve-hour adult lifespan; care boosts or Creative maturity are needed to inspect that endpoint within the default adult life.

First evolution still chooses the greatest earned points among Swim, Fly, Run and Power, with the most recently actually increased tied ability winning. Stamina, Intelligence and Luck do not compete. Untouched zero training selects Normal. Adult alignment and first type stay locked. No new saved field, migration, stat authority, lifecycle transition, network payload or server permission is introduced.

Natural color regions follow the six reference forms. Named, monocolor and jewel coats retain their underlying pigment and existing shiny finish; the same geometry serves every coat. Four unused semantic atlas texels supply body/tip/wing pigments. Facial pixels and source alpha remain protected elsewhere, and the original marker pigment is protected from inherited body repainting. Native review caught a copied texture-prefix mismatch; the corrected material registration and palette guards now share `dynamic/neutral_fly061/`, checked by the actual final render callback.

Full-head walking clearance retains the original gaze range and shoulder X anchors. This profile uses the existing 0.35-pixel head lift, a top-only 0.45-pixel torso extension and a 0.60-pixel downward arm-parent offset at full upright gait, fading with activity. It skips the older Power arm X translation. Strict checks caught and corrected the initial palm/head intersection without relaxing collision limits. Previously delivered family arithmetic remains unchanged.

## Boundaries and garden testing

The blocky crests, three-ray wings, flat color bands, fixed skull proportions, estimated colors and bilinear interior remain first-pass approximations. Balanced crests are shorter/blunter than the viewer's smooth taper, Run curls use two hard steps, and pure Fly fans are narrower and more separated with coarse white/yellow bands. All six identities were recognizable in the final comparison. Fresh Power and Power+Fly can lean or raise their arms while the viewer stands still: settings and approximate angles match, animation phases are not synchronized. Static photos are suspended for framing and use equal Run/Power mobility training to preserve net shape while enabling the ordinary upright pose. Camera phases in moving scenes occur at different ticks. Review scopes overlap; not every moving frame was visually inspected.

Water coverage is stationary in-water animation. Equipped carrying covers upright Creative flight, with the player obscuring some head/marker views. Autonomous gliding/swim travel, all accessory/animal/toy combinations, horizontal elytra/swimming carry and takeoff/landing remain unverified. The twelve formal reaction checks do not prove three distinct visible reaction faces in actual client captures. Physical touch hardware, independent remote multiplayer/latency/reconnect, full world/server restart, arbitrary old worlds, shaders/full modpacks, listening and full natural-lifetime pacing remain untested. The silent recording does not establish audio timing or full interactive playback.
