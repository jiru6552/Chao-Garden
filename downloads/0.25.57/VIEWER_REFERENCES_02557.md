# Chao Island viewer — Hero/Normal foundation .57

Fresh live research on 2026-11-12. The page returned HTTP200 and a real 3D Chao rendered in Chromium156. The sandbox browser initially failed ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID. The same verified-TLS browser succeeded in the outer executor using the existing CA trust; verification was not disabled. No compatibility warning appeared. No desktop CUA tool was exposed, so Playwright operated the actual Unity canvas by mouse clicks/drags and the camera-reset key.

## Settings and scope

The Child checkbox was unchecked, Normal first evolution selected and Hero alignment moved to its maximum. Run/Power and Swim/Fly are independent opposed sliders. Their endpoints and representative intermediate positions were set on visible tracks. The age track is a separate input. Orbit drags produced distinct front, both profiles, back, above and below views. Recorded track fractions are approximate visible-control positions, not extracted authoritative game values. No viewer geometry or morph implementation was copied.

These are **Hero / Normal first evolution / later specialization** forms. They differ from the already implemented **Hero Run first-evolution** path. In particular all nine retain TWO top features; the Hero Run first type's centered three-branch mature crest is not the construction reference for this lattice.

The white head and body remain dominant across this natural Hero family. Feet have a large white side/back portion with colored toe regions. The body back also receives the respective accent colors. Broad bands are visible in the reference; the mod can interpret them as a few pixel rows rather than extra decorative geometry. The halo is a suspended ring above both top features. No third central horn or separate Run-first-type front nubs were seen in these Normal-first-type forms.

The smooth reference skull changes somewhat between forms; the mod deliberately keeps the approved original 7×6×6 cuboid head fixed and translates these silhouettes into attached blocky features. Specific box sizes, exact RGB values and texture-band widths are authored simplifications, not measurements claimed from the viewer.

## Young forms and intermediate development

All nine young states were actually inspected from front, side and back. They share two short cyan buds, a pale-cyan halo, white head/body, yellow belly and wings, and blue hands/feet, regardless of the current training endpoints. All nine half-age states were also inspected from these three angles: their paired additions and color placement are visibly intermediate. The quarter- and three-quarter-age side views of all nine were reviewed as well.

At fixed mature age, Run, Run+Fly, Power+Swim and Fly were independently set to25%,50%,75% training positions and inspected from front, side and back. These alter the mature buds/stalks and regional colors without changing the biological age control. Fly training shrinks the centered mature buds toward smaller round caps; Run extends them; paired influences retain two features rather than inventing a third. Exact interpolation timing, RGB values and mathematical curves are not established from these screenshots.

The completed session retained **166 unchanged original PNGs**: one initial Neutral child page check, three initial Hero render/change/orbit checks,54 mature endpoint views (nine forms ×six angles),27 young views,27 half-age views,18 quarter/three-quarter-age side views and36 fixed-age training-quarter views. All166 were personally inspected as originals or uniformly cropped/resized diagnostic contact sheets. Diagnostic composites are separate and are not counted as original screenshots.

## Existing official assets

The old addon Hero, Hero Fly, Hero Power and baby geometry were inspected as existing cuboid construction records. `official-construction-inspection.json` retains exact source hashes and selected skull/marker cube definitions. Hero Fly and Hero Power are different first-type models; they are not direct replacements for the Hero/Normal mixed specializations. The converted `official/chao_hero.json` and retained textures are construction/color-placement context rather than permission to copy the viewer mesh.

## Limits

The viewer does not expose all inherited Chao coat colors or shiny finishes. Those need separate mod checks. It does not establish absolute Minecraft stat thresholds or elapsed maturity timing. These observations concern actual canvas views, not polygon correspondence, copied geometry or a proved original-game growth formula.
