# Actual Hero/Normal material review — 0.25.57

Final-cohort review covers all nine mature Hero/Normal forms from front, both sides, back, above and below, fresh/half/mature centered examples, inherited finishes, a vanilla carved pumpkin, rabbit replacements and retained Hero baby orb states. Original image hashes and literal capture lines are in `review-source-index.json`; contact sheets use one uniform crop/resize, with no altered geometry, colors or retouch.

## Observations

- The unchanged white blocky head/body remain coherent. Native face pixels stay aligned and compact hands/feet retain clear colored caps and broad bands. White heel/side areas remain visible.
- Centered maturity develops the pale-cyan halo toward blue while preserving yellow belly/wings. Run moves to a cyan halo, blue belly and blue/green extremities. Run+Fly has a muted mint/gray halo and lavender belly; Run+Swim has mint halo/cyan belly/green bands.
- Power shows the distinct fuchsia halo, gold belly and purple/pink bands. Power+Fly has pink halo, ochre belly and lavender/pink bands. Power+Swim has pink halo, lime belly and cyan/blue accents.
- Fly shows salmon halo, pale peach belly and subdued lavender accents; Swim shows pale-yellow halo, mint belly and cyan/green accents. These agree with the observed viewer color families, while the exact RGB values and broad pixel rows are authored approximations.
- Halo faces use coherent pigment; dark undersides and side surfaces follow normal Minecraft face lighting. Red shiny additionally shows the existing silver reflection across the body and halo. That reflection is not an atlas hue mismatch and was deliberately preserved.
- Ruby with phoenix/rabbit parts and glasses remains recognizable and aligned. The pumpkin fits the original head box and hides crown additions using the existing decoration rule; the colored halo remains above it. Rabbit parts keep their own original textures and hide the head additions as intended.
- The retained Hero baby cases show the intentional pink maximum-Run orb versus blue centered-Run orb. Their age/alignment/Run colors remain coherent through the sampled six angles.

## Limits

This is an actual static-image material review, not a claim of continuous playback, audio listening, physical phone input, shaders, independent multiplayer or full modpack testing. Static fixtures are suspended/frozen for underside photography and are not a ground-contact or natural-lifetime test. The viewer does not verify named or shiny coats; inherited color/finish behavior is the mod's authored policy. Some below views naturally occlude the halo or wings. Broad blocky bands and small square torso patches intentionally simplify the reference's smooth gradients. No blocking material issue was observed in these reviewed scenes.
