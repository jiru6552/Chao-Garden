## Observed result

The common newly evolved state converges correctly across all nine later influences. At 50% maturity and at maturity, the forms separate through rear-crest direction/length, palette, wings, extremities and marker color. The original rectangular head, rectangular eyes and simple closed limbs remain visibly consistent. I did not find an obvious detached crest, head/torso daylight gap, broken eye plane, stretched hand/foot, floating wing root or marker collision in these static samples. This is a bounded visual observation, not an every-frame collision guarantee.

The strongest intentional differences are the unchanged head box, simple compact rectangular body/limbs, pixel-U chest marking in place of a soft crescent, cube-based spiked marker, and compact stepped wing tabs. Wing colors are recognizable, but the Minecraft wings are less spread and less scalloped than the reference's bat webs. These are first-pass style approximations; they are not claims of exact source anatomy or gradients.

The fixed-maturity compatible-pair path is also deliberately approximate: actual viewer pair controls visually reach their paired endpoint at around 50% of both axes. A bilinear mod interior does not reproduce that exact progression rate. These three main stage sheets use full later influence and therefore do not independently establish the fixed-age quarter-training transition shape.

## Limits of this review

The comparison settings and camera directions match approximately, with different rendering, lighting and poses. Mature balanced Minecraft has a different idle lean and arm pose; no image was warped or mirrored to disguise that difference. Static actors are suspended for the below-camera fixture, not shown as proof of natural hovering.

I did not review motion, every transition frame, UI interaction, color/shiny variants, animal parts, hats, toy grips, equipped-player carrying, permissions, save/reload or dedicated-server behavior in this static comparison pass. The coordinator and other audits cover those separately. No production source, model or original screenshot was changed by this review.
