# Neutral056 actual static forms visual audit

PASS with documented approximations. Reviewed **33 actual client cases, 198 original PNGs**, through all six captured angles. All eleven contact sheets use one fixed crop and uniform resize; original PNG hashes are recorded in audit.json.

No blocking visual defect was seen: head/eyes remain coherent, hands/feet compact, crest roots connected and crown/orb clear. This is independent static-image inspection, separate from source/emitter tests.

- **Neutral/Normal/Normal** — One compact upright central crown; half-age and mature crest/cap/limb paint develop without changing the box face. Young/half/mature body and limbs remain simple closed-looking blocks.
- **Neutral/Normal/Run** — Same fresh form; developed crown visibly leans rearward in left/right views. Hands and feet stay compact, with only modest foot length change.
- **Neutral/Normal/Run+Fly** — Crown progressively lowers into a small raked hump. Fly contribution suppresses the bright cap area; no paired horns or Run-first-type nubs were added.
- **Neutral/Normal/Run+Swim** — Taller rearward hook than Run+Fly, with green upper tip. Both side views show connected stepped cuboids; no visible separation at the roots.
- **Neutral/Normal/Power** — Slim upright crown becomes taller. Slightly stockier compact hands/body remain coherent; no long arm segments or distorted face.
- **Neutral/Normal/Power+Fly** — Small low curled cap/nub at maturity, distinct from tall Power. Compact hands, feet and face remain intact in all six views.
- **Neutral/Normal/Power+Swim** — Longer upright/back-leaning crown develops gradually; green tip stays above face and below orb. Limb changes are modest.
- **Neutral/Normal/Fly** — Mature forward/down curl is translated into three angled closed boxes. Side views show a deliberate open arch under the curl above the fixed head; this is an approximation, not a tear or detached part. Case22 front/below captured a native blink; young/mature eyes are open and aligned.
- **Neutral/Normal/Swim** — Single narrow back-hook develops with age. Crest and cap remain connected and clear of the face; no extra forehead nubs or paired horns.
- **Neutral/Normal/Run+Fly, fixed mature age, training25/50/75** — Independent training progression lowers/rakes the crown without shrinking the biological body. Three sampled intermediate shapes are readable; static captures alone do not establish temporal smoothness.
- **Neutral/Normal/Power+Swim, fixed mature age, training25/50/75** — Crown grows upward/back from the mature Normal baseline; compact parts and eyes remain intact through all three sampled influences.

The fixed head deliberately changes the viewer interpretation: smooth skull slopes become a small central cuboid crest. Mature Fly has an open angled arch over the fixed head, a clear approximation suitable for later polish. Wings remain the original planar pink parts. Young forms intentionally share one small crown; some mature differences are subtle.

The Neutral orb is visually warm gold/peach when young and yellow with maturity, consistent across specialization at the same age. This audit does not cover Hero baby orb behavior. Case22 was captured during a native blink; young and mature Fly eyes are open/aligned.

Static fixture suspension and varied native poses limit ground/contact claims and projected-size comparisons. Motion, actual accessories/toys/carry, arbitrary shaders and multiplayer are outside this audit.
