## Deliberate approximations and limits

The mod preserves the fixed original head box and interprets the broad cap with a low stepped top and broad rectangular rear hem/angled rear piece. Its rear projection is more rod-like and less tapered than the smooth viewer silhouette. Each fin uses two simple blocks with flat bands. Front-facing fin bands read less strongly than the viewer because the block fins can be edge-on. Body bulk differences and curved/gradient regions are less pronounced than in the source viewer. These are reported foundation approximations, not newly discovered wrong directions or attachment failures. Compatible-mixture/bilinear development rates remain an authored mod approximation rather than a claim to reproduce the viewer's hidden formula.

Static samples do not establish every intervening frame, movement clipping, UI permissions, networking, save/reload or evolution-selection correctness. This audit does not replace the separate native motion and rules tests.
