## Existing opposing behavior

The delivered Neutral Run-first adult did not enter the scoped Hero/Run, Neutral/Normal, Hero/Normal, Neutral/Swim, Dark/Normal or Neutral/Fly profile. Its secondary labels therefore fell through to the saved `swimFly` and `runPower` fields. The generic production renderer sampled both signed axes with its mature 3×3 targets.

`ChaoState.influence(stat, amount)` gives Swim and Run negative deltas and Fly and Power positive deltas. It updates the appropriate current influence and passes it through `GradualEvolutionRules.ordinaryAxis`, which clamps only to `[-1,+1]`. There is no first-Run restriction at zero. Power training can cancel negative Run, reach balance and continue to positive Power while `normalizeEvolutionState()` restores the locked first type and alignment. Fly and Swim similarly cross their shared axis. A pair of opposing influences is one signed coordinate, rather than two simultaneous forms.

Absolute Creative stat edits set current bars while older saved influence fields can retain a different history. The new scoped policy deliberately follows current `100 × level + progress` bars for labels, geometry and materials, as the previously completed scoped families do. It does not overwrite the old saved axes. This makes normal/combined gains, losses and absolute edits converge on the same reversible target.

A separate actual viewer session independently corroborated a valid Power endpoint while Run remained the selected first-type checkbox. That is visual reference evidence, not a derivation of the game's source logic. Full viewer settings/actions and original screenshots are owned by the viewer audit.
