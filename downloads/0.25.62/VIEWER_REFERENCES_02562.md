## What was inspected

All nine later states were checked while keeping first type Run and alignment Neutral: balanced, Run, Power, Fly, Swim, Run+Fly, Run+Swim, Power+Fly and Power+Swim. Pair order represents the same compatible combination. Each has fresh/half/mature front, face-right side and approximately square-back views, quarter/three-quarter-age sides, and mature opposite-side/above/below views. Every nonbalanced direction also has mature fixed-age training at approximately 25%, 50% and 75%. Actual mouse-orbit deltas were +100 pixels for face-right side, +180 for a visually near-square rear, -100 for the opposite side, +75 vertical for above and -65 for below, resetting with R before each view. These are visually checked orientations, not calibrated camera degrees.

Age zero with the adult Run checkbox selected is the viewer's newly evolved Run form; it is not the initial Child checkbox example. All nine fresh states converge on the same lime-green adult shape and palette: central upward/backward crest, small rear-side points, **two separate front-side nubs above/outside the eyes**, small angled green wings and a short tail. Hands/belly are darker green, feet have gold/ochre accents and the floating orb is lime green. Three tear/drop-like marks run up the forehead and appear on each side in stronger green. The original source skull narrows and rounds, but the mod must retain its existing rectangular head and express these changes only through added blocky features and pixel regions.

## Opposite influences and intermediate development

**The opposite Power direction is possible in the viewer while first type remains Run.** The actual Run checkbox stays selected as the later Run/Power control moves from Run through centered balance to Power. Power, Power+Fly and Power+Swim are visibly distinct endpoints. A centered later control restores the balanced Run-first appearance, not the Neutral/Normal-first family. The mod's existing policy must be inspected separately; this viewer observation is not a reason to change its identity locks or biological age.

An early visual concern that paired controls had normalized to half-range was investigated and disproved. `slider-handle-proof.json` records exact opaque handle pixels from the original nine mature front screenshots. The blue handles are RGB `(0,98,198)`: center spans x743..772, left end x566..595 and right end x921..950. All four pairs retain both independently requested ends. No coupling/normalization is established. The reference manifest also records measured slider handles for every family image. Named track fractions remain approximate mouse positions and are not source-game stat levels or exact shared units with the mod.

Single-axis training changes visibly across 25/50/75% at a fixed mature age. All four compatible pairs look nearly at their combined endpoint by approximately 50% on each track; their 50/75/100% silhouettes and palettes are very similar even though the handles retain different values. This is an observation about appearance, not imported morph logic. An authored bilinear mod interior is an explicit first-pass approximation to that undocumented behavior.

Changing only age shows a gradual shared green fresh form developing into each endpoint. Balanced becomes blue mostly through pigment, nubs and small accessory-feature changes. Run bends and lengthens its hooks; Power turns its crests upward; Fly raises the rear blades; Swim broadens the rear/paddle shape. Intermediate green combined with the later magenta/blue produces muted tan, teal or greenish colors before maturity. It would be incorrect to apply the entire mature palette or geometry immediately at first evolution.

## Construction reference and limits

The existing imported-addon Run geometry, `run.png` texture and converted official model were inspected separately. Their hashes and exact source-skull cube are in `official-asset-review.json`; `official-asset-review.md` summarizes the stepped central two-cuboid crest and two mirrored two-cuboid rear spikes. The old asset supports the requested blocky construction vocabulary. Its raised face/brow replacement, zero-thickness wing cards and old green-only palette are not complete Neutral Run specialization references and should not replace the current face, closed surfaces or shared attachment rig.

No viewer geometry, normals, source model or morph code was imported. No Minecraft build, gameplay permission, native persistence, multiplayer, real accessory or movement test is established by this viewer research. The coordinator and implementation/test agents own those checks. Smooth head curvature, curved spike taper, joined wing lobes, exact gradients, stat units, timing and camera degrees remain interpretation limits. The mod's original box head, pixel eyes and coherent compact limbs take priority over reproducing smooth source anatomy.
