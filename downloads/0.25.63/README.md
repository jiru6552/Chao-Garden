The original 7 x 6 x 6 head box and all eight earlier gameplay models remain unchanged. Age develops independently of reversible training; adult alignment and first type stay locked. Build passed, all 526 dedicated-server tests passed, and the final client completed 93 cases with 796 original captures. The independently operated viewer and actual Minecraft forms are compared at matching ages, settings and angles in the report.
