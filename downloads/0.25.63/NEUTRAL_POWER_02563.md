## Opposing training and locked first identity

The delivered .62 generic Power-first implementation already allowed both complete signed axes. `ChaoState.influence()` adds a negative Run/Swim or positive Power/Fly delta and independently clamps ordinary axes to −1…+1. No generic Power rule stops later training at balance. The new path preserves that behavior rather than declaring opposite Run or its compatible pairs impossible. Existing scoped Swim059/Fly061 policies keep their own earlier restrictions unchanged.

`NeutralPowerMorphology063` reads current level bars: Run/Power is `(Power−Run)/960`, Swim/Fly is `(Fly−Swim)/960`, both clamped to −1…+1. A bar is the existing `100×level+progress`, bounded by the established maximum of 9900. Ordinary drives, all combined tiers, reductions and absolute Creative changes therefore select from the same current data. The older saved influence fields can disagree after absolute level editing; they remain intact. A saved Power adult whose current bars disagree with its accumulated legacy axes can display the current-bar specialization without changing first type, alignment or biological maturity.

The scoped profile requires a living mortal ordinary adult, normal character/variant, locked Neutral alignment strictly between −0.5 and +0.5 and locked first type Power (`POWER_TYPE=4`). Other first types, Hero/Dark families and special/Chaos characters retain their existing policies. Color, jewel and shiny genes do not enter these morphology coordinates.

First evolution still uses the greatest earned points among Swim, Fly, Run and Power, with the saved most recently actually raised tied ability winning. Stamina, Intelligence and Luck do not compete. An untouched record remains Normal. Adult alignment and first type remain locked; conflicting Creative alignment edits are rejected atomically. Later Power→Run and Fly→Swim changes cannot reset lifespan, reduce biological maturity or cause another first cocoon.
