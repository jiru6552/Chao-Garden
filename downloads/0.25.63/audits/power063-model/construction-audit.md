Power-first anatomy differs from the preceding Run family. It has one upper axial crown, one mid/lower axial rear point and two separate front-side horns. It does not reuse Run's paired rear hooks and tiny nubs. Nineteen closed cuboids share ten targets: eight head parts, five body/hand/foot boxes, four small wing lobes and two tail sections. Every target retains the same part topology and positive dimensions.

The first target is a common fresh adult: short crown and rear point, broad outward/upward horns and compact limbs. Mature balanced grows the crown upward/backward and lengthens the lower rear point. Later Run bends both axial points back/down. Full Power thickens the three upper points and shortens the lower rear point. Fly raises a tall crown whose terminal section bends **up/forward**, with thin upright side horns and a higher rear point. Swim flattens the upper point backward and folds its horns outward into small side fins. The four compatible paired targets use observed moderated shapes rather than stacking complete single endpoints. Age and the two signed training axes blend independently.
