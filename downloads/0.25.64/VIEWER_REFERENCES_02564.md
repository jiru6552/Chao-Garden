No page geometry or morph implementation was extracted.

I personally opened the default rendered Chao, selected the actual yellow Swim first-type checkbox, set full Hero alignment, changed age and both training controls, and rotated with R-reset and mouse orbit. Fresh front and side control-proof originals retain the full controls. The adult Swim checkbox remains selected when later Swim/Fly moves from full Swim through balance to full Fly. The Fly-directed endpoint has a distinct silhouette, not the balanced one.

The mature nine-form front/side/back sheet, opposite-side/above/below sheet, and natural palette sheet were personally opened. Full-size originals were additionally opened for fresh, pure forms and pairs. Age/training labels describe requested normalized handle positions, not source-game stat units. The source skull itself tapers and changes. That is not transferable to this project: keep the original head box fixed and use separate blocky crown pieces. Source toes are webbed and wing edges/paint use curves and zigzags; a small number of solid boxes and clean pixel regions is the appropriate interpretation. No claim of exact source morph formulas, geometry, timing, or calibrated degrees.

The family cohort covers all nine signed endpoints at ages 0,25,50,75,100%. Front, face-right side and approximately square back are captured at ages 0,50,100%; mature forms also have opposite-side, above and below views. Actual Hero/Dark/Neutral alignment changes were also performed for context; those context references do not expand this implementation family.

An independent image-pixel audit checked 600 opaque handle readings across 150 family originals. All selected Swim checkbox patches match the personally opened Swim-control proof. The maximum normalized absolute handle error is 0.03028169014084503 under 0.04 tolerance. Slider fractions and camera deltas are approximate; they are not source-game stat levels, calibrated degrees, biological-year timing or recovered morph formulas. Viewer Run-positive is the negative of the mod Run/Power axis; Fly-positive is the same sign as the mod Swim/Fly axis.

At fixed mature age, single later influences develop across quarter settings. Compatible pair silhouettes already look close to their paired endpoint around50% on both sliders; the actual full paired endpoints were additionally captured with both handles at their ends. This does not prove source control coupling or normalization. A shared fresh target and bilinear mature grid in the mod are deliberate first-pass approximations of this behavior. Source age 0 forms look very similar, but actor-crop RGB hashes are not byte-identical across controls; no exact source mesh/image identity is claimed.

The source model is smooth and its underlying skull tapers. In this project the base head box must remain untouched. The reference suggests separate paired draping/upturned/paddle features, small closed wing/fins, simple feet and clean pixel accents. Exact webbed toes, facial/skull taper, curved ribbons, zigzag borders and source shader lighting should be simplified into the established Minecraft construction rather than reproduced polygon-for-polygon. Geometry, source code and source morph logic were not imported.
