# Hero Swim 0.25.64 material source audit

This new profile is isolated under `dynamic/hero_swim064/` and shares the current canonical baby atlas, retained animation rig and display-only thirty-tick interpolation. No saved field, new dependency or general-purpose renderer API is added. Biological maturity and the two reversible signed training axes are independent inputs; the fixed Hero/Swim-first identity is checked by the core policy owned separately.

I personally opened twenty-nine fresh actual viewer originals: the initial fresh front/side controls; mature balanced, Swim and opposite Fly front/back; mature Run and Power front/side/back; all four paired mature front/back views; fresh balanced back; and selected half-age balanced, Swim, Fly and Power front/side/back views. These are actual 0.25.64-session viewer images, not earlier images supplied by the user. The hash-bound JSON lists the exact files. I also personally opened four old imported atlases—Hero, HeroFly, HeroPower and Swim. No imported or converted Hero Swim-specific asset exists; the legacy generated gameplay scaffold is not treated as an official source model.

The actual fresh Hero Swim is materially different from Hero/Neutral: white skull, white central belly and rear heels, mint torso and mitts, pale-yellow accents and halo, white-to-mint drooping crown flaps and small blue/cream wings. Mature balanced develops a blue halo and turquoise torso, cream upper crown shoulders and teal terminals. Additional Swim becomes violet in the torso, halo and terminal flaps; opposite Fly stays Swim-first while developing green/lime regions, raised tips, yellow halo and yellow/cream wings. Run develops cyan/blue; Power develops mint/gold paddles and mitts with an orange halo. Pair endpoints are not interchangeable monotints: Run+Fly has aqua/lime, Run+Swim periwinkle/mint with a pale gray-green halo, Power+Fly light green/lime and gold, and Power+Swim slate/tan/mint with a dusty pink halo.

The natural head remains the original source white throughout. Colored torso paint is restricted to the six original torso-face islands; the front white belly is surrounded by a compact flat contrasting pixel rim. Mitts and toes use variant colors with a simple pixel edge. The original tail stays white or inherited coat-colored; no pink old-family base is introduced. RGB values and compact boundaries are authored visual estimates. Smooth source gradients, zigzags, soft belly curves and multiple intermediate cap colors are simplified to a few flat regions.

Exactly five native source-transparent texels become opaque: `(127,63)` feature root, `(125,63)` upper-cap proximal color, `(126,63)` terminal color, `(124,63)` wing root and `(123,63)` wing end. All 172 original canonical PNGs have alpha zero at all five coordinates: 860 source samples. Two cap colors are painted across the longitudinal faces of each single upper cuboid, rather than adding band meshes. Wing lobes use the same two-region method with their independent swatches. Facial and reaction pixels and all other source alpha remain protected. No Run stripe tile or original skull marking is added.

Named, monocolor and jewel variants use the same geometry and UV boundaries with inherited pigments and bounded value contrast; their underlying color never becomes the natural white/mint scheme. The halo pigment depends only on maturity and both training axes, independently of skin, inherited accents and shiny genes. The existing shiny path re-renders the actual model through `ChaoShine.surface(OfficialChaoRig.texture(state))`, so the new dynamic material and five opaque feature texels feed the existing reflection alpha. The raw overlay source belongs to the separate animal/variant/flame rig. This is a source trace, not a claim of visibly checked moving highlights.

Thirty retained material, appearance and shiny source files are byte-identical to delivered 0.25.63. Root owns the minimal shared dispatch/mask hooks; no previous family source is changed by this agent. The new material main samples all 172 coats, actual current cap and wing face interiors, original skull and tail islands, source-independent halo, palette masks and one-percent progression. Its source audit does not predeclare any build or native-client result. Actual execution and visual acceptance are separate evidence.

The first material run exposed an overly strict test boundary: the retained emotion-dot UV has a legal vertex exactly at the outer atlas edge. The test now permits face corners at that edge while keeping every sampled face interior strictly within `[0,128) x [0,64)`. This is a test-only correction; no product geometry or paint changed to satisfy it. The earlier failure is retained privately and must not be counted as a passed run.
